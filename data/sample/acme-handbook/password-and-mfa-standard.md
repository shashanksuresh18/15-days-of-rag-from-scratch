## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use a passphrase or the company password manager to generate one. Never reuse your Acme password on another website.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, the VPN and all internal applications. Use the authenticator app on your company phone or a hardware security key. Never share a one-time code with anyone, including people who claim to be from IT.

## Resetting your password
Reset a forgotten password with the self-service portal at https://it.acme.example/reset. You need your registered MFA device to complete the reset.

## Account lockout
Your account locks after five failed sign-in attempts. It unlocks automatically after 15 minutes, or the IT Service Desk can unlock it sooner after verifying your identity.
