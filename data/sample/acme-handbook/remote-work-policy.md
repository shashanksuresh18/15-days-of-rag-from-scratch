## Purpose
This policy explains how Acme employees can work remotely while staying productive, connected and secure. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors follow the terms in their contract.

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their probation period. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your manager and the People Team. Some roles, such as lab and warehouse roles, require on-site work.

## Core hours
Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone. Outside core hours you can arrange your schedule with your manager.

## Equipment and home office
Employees approved for remote work receive a one-time home office allowance of USD 500 for a desk, chair or monitor. Claim the allowance through the expense system within 60 days of approval. Acme provides the laptop; personal computers must not be used for company work.

## Security while remote
Connect through the company VPN when you access internal systems from outside the office. Do not work on confidential documents in public places where your screen can be seen.
