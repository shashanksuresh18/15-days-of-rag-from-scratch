## Your first day
Collect your laptop and security badge from reception. Sign in with the temporary password sent to your personal email, then set a new password that follows the password standard.

## Accounts and access
Set up multi-factor authentication before you open email. Request access to team tools through the access request form; your manager approves each request.

## Training
Complete the security awareness and data protection courses within your first two weeks.

## Getting help
Contact the IT Service Desk through the self-service portal or the #it-help chat channel.
