## Scope
This policy covers business expenses that employees pay personally and claim back from Acme, including travel, meals and approved purchases.

## Submitting a claim
Submit expense claims in the expense system within 30 days of the purchase. Attach an itemised receipt for every expense over USD 25. Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed unless Finance grants an exception.

## Approval and payment
Your manager approves claims under USD 1,000; claims above that also need Finance approval. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Travel
Book flights and hotels through the travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Ground transport such as trains, buses and rideshares is reimbursed at cost.

## Meals
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to USD 75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
