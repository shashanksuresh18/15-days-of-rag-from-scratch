## What counts as an incident
A security incident is any event that may put Acme data or systems at risk. Examples include clicking a suspicious link, a lost or stolen laptop, a phishing email, or data sent to the wrong person.

## How to report
Report suspected incidents to security@acme.example or call the Security hotline within one hour of noticing them. Report even if you are unsure; false alarms are welcome.

## What not to do
Do not try to investigate the incident yourself, delete evidence or forward suspicious emails to colleagues. Disconnect a possibly infected laptop from the network but do not turn it off.

## After you report
The Security Team will confirm receipt, assess the impact and tell you what to do next. Incidents involving personal data may have to be reported to regulators, so speed matters.
