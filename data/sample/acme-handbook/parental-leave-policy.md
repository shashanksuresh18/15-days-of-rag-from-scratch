## Who is eligible
All employees with at least six months of service are eligible for parental leave when they become a parent through birth, adoption or surrogacy.

## Leave entitlement
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the child's arrival.

## How to request leave
Tell your manager and submit a request in the HR portal at least eight weeks before you plan to start your leave, where possible.

## Returning to work
You may return on a phased schedule of reduced hours for up to four weeks without a change in pay.
