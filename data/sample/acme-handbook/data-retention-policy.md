## Purpose
This policy sets how long Acme keeps different types of records and when they must be deleted. Keeping data longer than needed increases legal and security risk.

## Retention periods
Customer data is deleted within 90 days after a contract ends, unless the law requires longer retention. Financial records are kept for seven years. Application logs are kept for 13 months.

## Deletion requests
Forward any customer request to delete personal data to privacy@acme.example within two business days. Do not delete data yourself until Legal confirms the request.

## Legal holds
When Legal issues a legal hold, the affected records must not be deleted, even if their retention period has ended.
