## When to use the VPN
Use the VPN whenever you access internal systems, such as the finance system, file shares or the HR portal, from outside an Acme office. Public websites and email do not need the VPN.

## Installing the client
The VPN client is pre-installed on Acme laptops. If it is missing, install it from the Software Center; do not download VPN software from the internet.

## Connecting
Open the VPN client, choose the region closest to you and sign in with your Acme account and MFA. The connection icon turns green when you are connected.

## Troubleshooting
If the VPN does not connect, restart the client and check your internet connection. If the problem continues, contact the IT Service Desk with a screenshot of the error message.
